iDigBio is pursuing its final year of NSF funding with a scheduled termination of 31 August 2026. However, we have been approved for a no-cost-extension, which will allow us to continue into 2027.

We are in the process of transitioning and sunsetting iDigBio's current services, including its website, wiki, portal, and API. Note that this only affects iDigBio services and NOT any of the [Symbiota network portals](https://symbiota.org/symbiota-portals/).

Effective 20 November 2026, this website, our [GBIF Hosted Portal](./), is the new face of iDigBio. For the foreseeable future, all traffic to iDigBio.org will redirect here and all traffic to the legacy iDigBio Portal will redirect to the search integrated in this hosted portal. Access to iDigBio's community resources have been preserved through the following mechanisms, among others:
- [iDigBio's Events Hub](https://ufl.zoom.us/ze/hub/iDigBio), which hosts details of our upcoming webinars, conferences, and other online events,
- [iDigBio's YouTube Page](https://www.youtube.com/@iDigBio), which hosts recordings of our events and webinars,
- <img src="{{ site.data.images.InternetArchive.src }}" alt="{{ site.data.images.InternetArchive.alt }}" title="{{ site.data.images.InternetArchive.caption }}" style="margin: 1em 1em 1em 1em; float: right; width: 175px;">An [archive of iDigBio's legacy website and wiki](https://wayback.archive-it.org/17494/idigbio.org) created using the Internet Archive's Archive-It service,
- and Migration of all content and accoutns from the legacy iDigBio IPT to the [GBIF U.S. IPT](https://ipt.gbif.us/).

We appreciate your patience and support as we work through these transitions!
